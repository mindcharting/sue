Redesigning Navigational Framework for a Claims Enterprise • DecisionPoint

DecisionPoint’s side navigation had grown without a governing structure ie. global and contextual systems co-existing without clear hierarchy, making every new feature a negotiation with ambiguity. I led the re-architecture from the ground up: defining a five-layer hierarchy, unifying the claim tree, and documenting the interaction principles that let the system scale gracefully as the product grows.
